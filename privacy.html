Don't Look — Privacy Policy

Don't Look does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The game does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics, and does not transmit gameplay data to external servers.

Local Data

Game progress, settings, statistics, and save data are stored locally on your device.

Internet Connection

Don't Look can be played without an internet connection.

Don't Look — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Don't Look can be played fully offline without an account. Signing in is optional and is used only to back up your save data to the cloud, so your progress is not lost when you change or reinstall your device. We do not use advertising or analytics, we do not track you, and we do not sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, statistics, and save data are stored locally on your device. If you never sign in, nothing is sent to any server.

Data we collect if you sign in

When you choose to sign in (with Apple, Google, or email and password), we collect:

  • Email address — from your Apple, Google, or email sign-in (if you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's private relay address). Used to identify your account.
  • User ID — an anonymous identifier created for your account.
  • Save data — your game progress and the state of your current run.

This data is used only to provide cloud save and account features. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it is never sold or shared with third parties. For email sign-in, your password is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.

Service providers

Account and save data are stored with Supabase (our backend provider). Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are provided by Apple and Google, whose own privacy policies apply to their sign-in services.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and your cloud save from our server. Progress stored on your device is kept. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

Don't Look is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact: detourbyjason@gmail.com

Don't Look — 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 13 日

概要

Don't Look 不需要帳號也能完整離線遊玩。登入為選擇性功能， 只用來把您的存檔備份到雲端，讓您換手機或重新安裝後進度不會遺失。 本遊戲不使用廣告或分析工具、不追蹤您，也不會販售或分享您的資料。

儲存在您裝置上的資料

遊戲進度、設定、統計資料與存檔儲存在您的裝置上。 若您從未登入，不會有任何資料傳送到伺服器。

登入後我們蒐集的資料

當您選擇登入（使用 Apple、Google，或電子郵件與密碼）時，我們會蒐集：

  • 電子郵件地址：來自您的 Apple、Google 或電子郵件登入 （若您使用 Apple 的「隱藏我的電子郵件」，我們只會收到 Apple 的私人轉寄地址）， 用於識別您的帳號。
  • 使用者 ID：為您的帳號建立的匿名識別碼。
  • 存檔資料：您的遊戲進度與目前進行中的一局狀態。

這些資料只用於提供雲端存檔與帳號功能，不會用於廣告、分析或追蹤， 也不會販售或分享給第三方。使用電子郵件登入時，您的密碼由驗證服務以雜湊方式儲存， 我們看不到您的密碼。

服務供應商

帳號與存檔資料儲存於 Supabase（我們的後端服務供應商）。 「使用 Apple 登入」與 Google 登入分別由 Apple 與 Google 提供， 其登入服務適用各自的隱私權政策。

刪除帳號與資料

您可隨時在遊戲內刪除帳號： 設定 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號。 這會永久刪除您的帳號與雲端存檔；儲存在您裝置上的進度會保留。 您也可以來信要求刪除。

兒童

Don't Look 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新上方日期。
